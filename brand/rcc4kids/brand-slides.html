RCC4Kids Brand Book12 / 15

Safeguards · 1 of 2

Falsifiable bans. If a file breaks one of these, it is not RCC4Kids.

01

Never spell the public brand RCC4kids, RCC 4 Kids, RCCforkids, or rcc4kids in display type — it is RCC4Kids.

02

Never set body copy or captions in #0AF50E, #E5E907, or #EF0B0B. Those colors are blocks and marks, not text.

03

Never put white type on playground green or yellow. Both carry ink #1A1714.

04

Never use Unsplash, Adobe Stock, or generic daycare photography. The live site’s current slides are the example of what not to do.

05

Never generate or retouch photorealistic children’s faces.

06

Never write kiddos, little ones, where magic happens, or premier daycare.

RCC4Kids Brand Book13 / 15

Safeguards · 2 of 2

The rest of the immune system.

07

Never recolor, outline, or rainbow-gradient anything except the owned flower mark.

08

Never give all four playground colors equal weight on one surface — pick one lead color.

09

Never use cool gray or blue-gray paper. Neutrals stay warm #FAF8F4.

10

Never claim Reggio Emilia, or invent waitlist times, tuition, or testimonials.

11

Never set Nunito as UI chrome. Display and headings are Nunito; body is Nunito Sans.

12

Never use sharp 0-radius corners on cards or buttons. Soft vibe is structural, not decorative.
